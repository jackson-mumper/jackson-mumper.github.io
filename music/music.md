# Music Journalism

I'm a huge nerd for all things pop music and the Billboard charts. In both 2024 and 2025, I listened to 150 new (to me) albums from over two dozen countries. I love learning about the different geographies of pop music, its various different regional styles, and quantifying trends based on a variety of different charts. I channel these interests into my Substack, mump's playlist, where I post a variety of chart analyses, song reviews, cultural commentary, and music recommendations, including lists of my favorite albums and hit singles of each year. *(include a bit here about music books I've read, music podcasts/YouTubers, the ways I try to learn more about the industry. this page is my best shot to sell myself to a Billboard employer)*
